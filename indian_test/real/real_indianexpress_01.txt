Minister of External Affairs S Jaishankar (FB@drsjaishankar)
India on Friday criticised the US suspension of the Permanent Labor Certification (PERM) programme for eight major IT companies, including Infosys, TCS and Wipro, saying the move does not advance the shared ambitions of the two countries. The Ministry of External Affairs also called US Vice President JD Vance’s description of foreign professionals as “indentured servants” unwarranted and deeply offensive, highlighting the contribution of Indian professionals to the US economy and innovation ecosystem.

Trains shut, metro closed in Delhi ahead of protest
All trains to New Delhi, including Rajdhani Express, express and mail trains, have been cancelled for Saturday ahead of the protests in national capital. The railways authorities have announced that all trains arriving to New Delhi and Hazrat Nizamuddin railway stations have been cancelled in view of the congestion due to October 10 protest. 57 metro stations will be shut in the national capital from 9 pm on Friday.

Markets in green despite US move on green card
Market indices Sensex and Nifty opened in green on Friday morning despite a US crackdown on a green card programme that is set to adversely affect Indian major IT firms. Tata Consultancy Services shares rose on Friday by 3.5 per cent at Rs 2,149.10 amid accelerating AI-related revenue and growth in its international business.
Major tech and IT outsourcing firms hit by the Trump administration’s move include Cognizant, Infosys, Tata, Wipro, HCL, Capgemini, Microsoft and Adobe. The move is a major blow for Indian professionals looking to work at some of these companies in the US.
Counting for 5 assembly, 1 Lok Sabha seats today; big test for Vijay, Suvendu Adhikari
Friday will see a high-stake contest as results will be declared for five Assembly seats and one Lok Sabha constituency across West Bengal, Tamil Nadu, Assam and Puducherry. The results from Nandigram and Rejinagar in West Bengal, Madurantakam and Dharapuram in Tamil Nadu, Thattanchavady in Puducherry and Assam’s Nagaon Lok Sabha seat will show how the BJP, Congress and regional parties have fared in these contests.
Central forces called as Delhi braces for protests
Amid continuing protests against Chief Election Commissioner Gyanesh Kumar, Delhi police took a string of security measures — 23,000 Central forces personnel have been called to assist the city police, five all-women paramilitary companies will be on duty, liquor vends will be closed for two days and Central Delhi hotels have been asked to restrict room bookings. Opposition parties and civil society groups have called for a protest in capital on October 10.
