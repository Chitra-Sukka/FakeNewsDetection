Residents wept with relief as rescue boats finally reached the marooned village on Saturday after three days without food or clean water. "We thought nobody would come," said a farmer who had spent two nights on his roof with his family and two goats. Officials said more than 2,000 people had been moved to safety and that relief supplies, including drinking water and medicines, were being sent to the area. Volunteers from nearby towns had been cooking meals since Friday night.
