Civil society groups in Karnataka have been praised for documenting alleged irregularities in the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls. Addressing a press briefing, political activists alleged mass detentions of youth associated with the movement across several States ahead of the October 10 protest at Delhi’s Jantar Mantar. They urged people nationwide to join the peaceful demonstration, accusing the government of ignoring their demands and calling for accountability from the Election Commission.
